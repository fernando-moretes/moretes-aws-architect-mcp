# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is a short form; the linked text is the full one and prevails.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Portuguese and English are both welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or e-mail address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at **fernando@moretes.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this code, and may issue a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
